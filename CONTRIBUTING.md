# Contributing to Demiourgos

Thanks for your interest in contributing!

For build, test, and lint commands, and the conventions this codebase follows,
see [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md). In short: `cargo test` for the unit suite,
`cargo clippy --all-targets -- -D warnings` for the lint gate, and
`cargo fmt --all` before you commit.
